# Credits

Every line in The Reader's Eye is a short excerpt from a published young adult novel. The page shows each quote with its book and author, both in the caption as you read the eye and in the list "Every quote in this eye".

The page quotes 24 lines from 15 books:

- *Six of Crows* by Leigh Bardugo (1 quote)
- *The Perks of Being a Wallflower* by Stephen Chbosky (3 quotes)
- *Catching Fire* by Suzanne Collins (1 quote)
- *Mockingjay* by Suzanne Collins (1 quote)
- *The Hunger Games* by Suzanne Collins (2 quotes)
- *Looking for Alaska* by John Green (2 quotes)
- *Paper Towns* by John Green (1 quote)
- *The Fault in Our Stars* by John Green (5 quotes)
- *The Outsiders* by S. E. Hinton (1 quote)
- *The Giver* by Lois Lowry (1 quote)
- *Wonder* by R. J. Palacio (1 quote)
- *Divergent* by Veronica Roth (1 quote)
- *Eleanor & Park* by Rainbow Rowell (1 quote)
- *The Hate U Give* by Angie Thomas (1 quote)
- *The Book Thief* by Markus Zusak (2 quotes)

## How the quotes are used

The quotes are short excerpts, each credited to its author, used as the material of a typographic artwork. They are not covered by this repository's licence: each belongs to its author and publisher.

If you are an author or publisher and want a quote removed, open an issue on this repository and it will be taken down.
